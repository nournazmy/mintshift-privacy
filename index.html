Mintshift is a single-player game. This policy explains how the game handles information.

Game progress and settings, such as in-game money, assets, upgrades, achievements, and preferences, may be stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to us by the game.

Mintshift does not require an online account and does not send gameplay data to external servers. Google Play and your device operating system may independently process information under their own privacy policies when you use their services.

5. Children's Privacy

Mintshift does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

6. Data Security

Game progress is stored locally, so its security depends partly on your device's security and settings.
